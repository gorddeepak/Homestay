- PNG app icons and favicons are included for direct use. Use the SVGs for the lockups; the site inlines them so the page's webfonts apply.

Typography
The editable SVG wordmark references Space Grotesk SemiBold, and the tagline references DM Sans. The website loads these fonts in its design system. If opening the SVG outside the website, a system sans-serif fallback may be used depending on installed fonts.

Tagline
“STAYS MADE SIMPLE” is included as an optional lockup. The logo also works without it at small sizes.
